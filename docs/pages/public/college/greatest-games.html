Greatest College Games

Close games between two top teams. A game qualifies when both teams were in the top quarter of that season's college ratings (and strong over the previous seasons) and the margin was 8 points or less. Game score = how good both teams were (their place in that season's ranking, 100 = #1, so every era counts equally) × how close it was (a 1-point game scores highest).
