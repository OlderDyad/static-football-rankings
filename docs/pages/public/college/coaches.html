College Head Coaches

Every head coach we have, FBS through NAIA. Two ways to judge a coach: Avg Rating — the average rating of his teams (seasons as sole head coach in completed, ranked seasons) — and Value Added — how much better his schools rated under him than in the five seasons before and after his tenure, averaged over his career. Sort by either column.

0 coaches · Updated
    Value Added compares each school with itself, so it rewards coaches who lifted a program, not just those who inherited a power. It needs at least two ranked seasons of the school before or after the tenure; current coaches are measured against the seasons before they arrived. Coaching records are most complete for FBS and FCS; many small-college seasons are still missing a coach. Era Pct = where his teams finished in each season's ranking of all college teams (100 = first, 0 = last), averaged; ratings have risen over time, so sort by Era Pct to compare coaches across eras. Titles = seasons his team finished #1 in McKnight's ratings.
