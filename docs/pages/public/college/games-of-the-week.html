College Games of the Week

Upcoming games between two top teams, projected to be close

Updated

Every level is rated on one scale. All lists the best games nationally; the level tabs rank each level's own games by where the teams stand within that level, so a big FCS or Division III game gets its due. How games are ranked

Game Index = how good both teams are × how close the game should be. Team strength comes from each team's rank (national rank on the All list, rank within the level on a level tab), on a scale where #1 = 100 and each step down the top of the table counts more than a step further down: #5 vs #15 is a big difference, #125 vs #135 a small one.

Projected margin = the difference between the two ratings, in points, plus a few points for the home team (none at neutral sites). The home-field figure is measured from college games played from 2004 to 2025.

Closeness = 1 − projected margin ÷ 30: a toss-up 1.00, 4 points 0.87, 8 points 0.73, 10 points 0.67. Games projected at more than 10 points are not listed.

Schedules come from wolferatings.com and are refreshed each week; games can move or be cancelled after this page is published. Early-season ratings rest on few games and change more from week to week.
