How "vs. expected" works

The top 100 are the highest current-season Combined Ratings in the country (11-man programs), from the latest weekly calculation.

For every game, the expected margin comes from both teams' ratings before that game (the last weekly rating before game week; last season's final rating before the first rating of the season):

Expected margin = (Team rating − Opponent rating) ÷ 0.958 ± 1.6 home field
vs. expected = Actual margin − Expected margin

A positive number means the team beat its expectation by that many points; negative means it fell short. The 1.6-point home-field figure is measured from 1.76 million games, 2004–2025; neutral-site games get none.

Updated Saturday and Sunday mornings from MaxPreps. Ratings themselves are recalculated weekly, so a team's expectation only changes once a week.
