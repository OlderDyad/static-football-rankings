How the line works

The line is what our ratings said before kickoff: the difference in the two teams' Combined Ratings from the week before the game, in points, plus 1.6 points for the home team (none at a neutral site), rounded to the half point. −7.5 means the team was favored by 7.5; +3.5 means it was a 3.5-point underdog; PK means even.

Ratings pick: the team the line favored is the ratings' pick to win. ✓ Right means that team won; ✗ Upset means the underdog won (no pick on a PK game). Under each team, Picks 9-1 counts its games this season: right picks, then upsets. The small number shows how far the result landed from the line (+13 vs line = won by 13 more than the line said).

Games played before this season's first weekly ratings have no line (—). Results update Saturday and Sunday mornings from MaxPreps; ratings and lines update weekly. This is a measure of how well the ratings predict games, not betting advice.
