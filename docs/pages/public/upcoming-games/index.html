How Games Are Ranked
The Game Index

This is the Greatest Games Pulse Rate Index applied before kickoff. A game scores highly when both teams are strong and the game is projected to be close.

Game Index = (Home Rating + Visitor Rating) × Closeness Multiplier

Ratings are each team's current-season Combined Rating from the most recent weekly calculation. The projected margin is the difference between the two ratings, in points, plus 1.6 points for the home team. That home-field figure is measured from 1.76 million games played from 2004 to 2025.

Who qualifies

Both teams must have a Combined Rating above 20 this season (roughly the top 9% nationally) and play 11-man football. Unlike Greatest Games, there is no five-year history requirement: a team having a great season qualifies even if it wasn't strong before.

Caveats

Schedules come from MaxPreps and can change after this page is published. Neutral-site games aren't identified, so every game gets the home-field adjustment. Early-season ratings rest on only a few games and move more from week to week.
